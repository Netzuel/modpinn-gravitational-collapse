Numerical data and pretrained examples

The MIT licence at the repository root applies to the software and its
software documentation. The numerical reference data retain their original
citation-based distribution terms:

Use and distribution of the data are permitted, and reproduction in any medium
is allowed, provided that an appropriate citation is given.

The numerical simulations are attributed to Matthew W. Choptuik. The ModPINN
software and trained examples are attributed to Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez and the
associated paper. Preserve these attributions when redistributing these files.

Paper: https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae5459
Public numerical-data release: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18687035
Foundational numerical work: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.70.9

The bundled HDF5 files are the processed reference arrays used by the paper's
training scripts. The external data release is a citation, not a bundled
sub-repository or a runtime download dependency. No new licence is asserted
for third-party numerical data.
